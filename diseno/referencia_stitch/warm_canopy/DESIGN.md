## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a grounding, tactile, and playful connection to nature. Built around earthy warmth and tangible ergonomics, it discards sterile, clinical interfaces and stark white canvases in favor of an inviting forest-inspired atmosphere. 

### Key Characteristics
- **Personality:** Warm, down-to-earth, inventive, tactile, and delightfully approachable.
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Tactile Modernism meets Playful Organic Minimalism. Soft pillowy geometry, rich tonal layering, and physical visual metaphors create surfaces that feel pressable and natural.
- **Audience & Emotional Response:** Designed for individuals seeking calm clarity without corporate detachment. The interface elicits comfort, gentle curiosity, and a grounded sense of security—like handcrafted wooden objects set against mossy forest floors.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an immersive, muted sage-forest base, completely free from stark white backgrounds. High-touch navigational and interactive components pop with an energetic warm mustard, anchored by organic espresso and timber tones.

### Palette Roles
- **Canvas & Surface Base (Sage / Warm Forest Neutral - `#2B382E`):** The primary atmosphere for the entire viewport. Secondary surfaces shift slightly upward into warm olive-sage tints (`#344338`, `#3D4E41`) to establish distinct content planes without resorting to bright backdrops.
- **Primary Accent (Warm Mustard / Golden Honey - `#E5A93C`):** Highlights high-priority triggers, featured states, and the prominent floating navigation dock. Delivers an optimistic, sun-dappled contrast against the sage background.
- **Secondary Accent (Earth Timber / Café - `#5A3825`):** Grounded deep wood tone reserved for structural borders, badges, tactile pressed states, and warm typographic contrasts.
- **Tertiary Accent (Soft Olive Lichen - `#8C9E7D`):** Gentle sage-green tone used for subtle chips, inactive pill tracks, secondary iconography, and auxiliary text.
- **Text & Foreground Shades:** Content avoids pure `#FFFFFF`. Primary readable text leverages warm oat/linen (`#F4EFE6`), secondary copy uses soft dried moss (`#BAC8B3`), and deeply inverted copy atop mustard adopts dark roast timber (`#23160E`).

## Typography

Typography balances deliberate editorial character with friendly legibility. 

- **Headlines (Epilogue):** Chosen for its punchy geometric form, idiosyncratic ink traps, and warm structural curves. It brings an artisanal, editorial flair to titles without feeling stuffy or overly formal.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Its generous counters, friendly apertures, and clean contemporary geometry ensure smooth reading across dense layouts on warm sage backgrounds.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Keep headline line heights tight to maintain optical density. Ensure body text maintains generous vertical space for effortless reading over non-white, tinted surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt rhythmic grid that flexes naturally across screen viewports while preserving an intimate, cocoon-like envelope.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Interactive triggers and primary surfaces span full bleed or paired widths. Safe bottom offset of `5rem` reserves breathing room above the floating warm mustard bottom bar.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Cards organize into balanced dual-column groupings.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at `1200px` centered width with `2.5rem` outer canvas padding.

### Rhythm & Padding
Spacing values maintain generous interior container padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`), allowing components to feel chunky, pillowy, and physically distinct from one another.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile, physical layering rather than standard cold drop shadows. Depth is communicated through clay-like dimensional offsets, rich earthy undertones, and warm perimeter bevels.

### Surface Stratification
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep, velvety sage green (`#2B382E`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Soft warm olive (`#354439`) elevated with a subtle dual-rim stroke: a 1px top highlight of soft lichen tint (`rgba(244, 239, 230, 0.08)`) and a bottom ground shadow tinted in rich café bark (`#1E2720`).
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Modals & Floating Shelves):** Lifted forest moss (`#3F5244`) supported by a warm downward ambient shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(22, 29, 24, 0.45)`).
- **Level 3 (Floating Mustard Navigation & Primary Floating Triggers):** Completely suspended element rendered in warm golden honey (`#E5A93C`). It features an extruded tactile rim: a solid offset shadow (`0 6px 0px #3D2619`) layered over a soft diffused cast (`0 14px 28px rgba(35, 22, 14, 0.3)`), delivering a pressable, tactile sensation.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts pillowy, organic geometry (`roundedness: 3`). Corners are ultra-soft and rounded to mirror organic pebbles, polished river stones, and smooth wooden carvings.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (Input fields, standard tiles):** `1rem` (16px) curvature.
- **Large Components (Hero containers, modals, layered sheets):** `2rem` (32px) curvature.
- **Full Pills (Buttons, chips, search pills, navigation capsules):** Full pill radius (`9999px`), maximizing playful ergonomics and finger-friendly targets.

## Components

### Bottom Navigation Bar (Signature Element)
- **Appearance:** A floating horizontal pill docked above the lower viewport edge. Coated in vibrant mustard (`#E5A93C`) with rich dark espresso (`#23160E`) iconography and typographic labels.
- **Interaction:** Nav items feature pill-shaped active state cutouts in deep timber brown (`#5A3825`), rendering the active icon in bright warm oat (`#F4EFE6`).
- **Tactile Quality:** 2px subtle perimeter highlight with a 4px bottom dimensional edge creating a tactile, hand-held console presence.

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons:** Bold mustard pills (`#E5A93C`) featuring dark timber text (`#23160E`). Upon active press, the button translates 2px downward with a reduced offset shadow, mimicking a physical mechanical button.
- **Secondary Buttons:** Tinted olive pill (`#3D4E41`) framed with a 1.5px warm timber outline (`#5A3825`) and linen text (`#F4EFE6`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary Buttons:** Linen text (`#F4EFE6`) with translucent lichen pill backdrops (`rgba(140, 158, 125, 0.15)`) on hover.

### Chips & Tags
- Compact full-pill items designed with a 1.5px border. Inactive chips use a quiet lichen tint (`rgba(140, 158, 125, 0.2)`) on dark sage; selected chips toggle into a vibrant mustard wash (`#E5A93C`) with chocolate wood lettering.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Fields are rendered with deep olive wells (`#253027`) inset within cards. The border utilizes an earthy timber tint (`#483223`) that expands into a 2px mustard focus outline with a soft golden glow. Placeholder copy rests in muted moss (`#8C9E7D`).

### Cards & Grouping Containers
- Content cards use warm olive-sage tiles (`#354439`) with generous internal padding (`space-lg`). Content partitions within cards utilize warm wood-tinted divider lines (`rgba(90, 56, 37, 0.35)`) rather than harsh monochrome rules.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Rounded organic forms. Checkboxes display squircle contours (`0.5rem`) with custom thick timber checkmarks when toggled to mustard.
- Toggle switches resemble smooth wooden tracks (`#253027`) holding a tactile golden pill thumb that glides horizontally with an elastic spring animation.
